The problem

Delivery riding is one of the most dangerous jobs in Korea, yet nobody measures riding behavior before a crash. Commercial trucks and buses have digital tachographs (DTG) and risk-driving analytics. Two-wheelers do not, and four-wheel scoring misreads normal leaning turns as dangerous.

4 years

Baemin's delivery logistics subsidiary has had the most approved industrial-accident claims of any workplace in Korea (2022 to 2025).

~20,000

Motorcycle traffic crashes per year in Korea, with about 500 deaths per year (2018 to 2022 average).

20 to 30%

Personal-injury premium cut pushed by Korea's Financial Supervisory Service motorcycle insurance reform (Dec 2025), without behavior data to price risk.

Sources: Korea Workers' Compensation and Welfare Service data via National Assembly, Korea Road Traffic Authority, Financial Supervisory Service (2025.12).

What RideScore delivers

One riding-data platform for the three groups that pay for two-wheeler safety.

Delivery platforms

Personalized safety training based on each rider's own risk patterns, plus real-time risk alerts.

Public agencies

Quantified accident-prevention effects by comparing risky-riding rates before and after training.

Insurers

A two-wheeler-specific safety score to price risk and limit losses from mandated premium cuts.

Technology

Built on peer-reviewed research on delivery-rider safety.

Collect

Low-cost IMU + GPS units mounted anywhere on the bike (under the seat, on the delivery box), with smartphone sensing in parallel.

Calibrate & detect

Two-stage self-supervised calibration restores the vehicle frame (gravity alignment error 7.65° to 1.99°, about 1/5 the compute of a Kalman filter). Three-layer detection: data-quality rules, time-series anomaly detection, and motorcycle-dynamics checks (friction circle, sideslip). Controlled-test F1 0.73.

Score & report

Rider safety score (0 to 100, 5 grades), link-level road risk map, and intervention effect analysis.
